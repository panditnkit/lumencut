USER MANUAL

How to Use LumenCut

Admin setup and the actual video-processing and publishing workflow.

01

Input Video / Stream Source

Upload an MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, or M4V, or submit a supported HTTPS YouTube, Twitch, or Kick URL.

URL Ingestion Supported links depend on the source platform and its access rules. Private or protected videos cannot be processed.
Local Upload Drag & drop local raw recordings (.mp4, .mkv, .mov) directly from your drive.
Audio-First Whisper transcription and video rendering run on your configured backend and may take several minutes.
02

Configure Engine Parameters

Choose the speech language and vertical layout. The backend runs FFmpeg and an open-source Whisper model.

Caption Style Choose between Emerald Mint (TikTok), Hormozi Gold, Cyber Lime, or Clean Minimal.
Speech Language Native support for Hinglish (Hindi written in English), pure Hindi, English, and 52+ languages.
Layout Mode Crop: Center-crops to 9:16. Blur: Fills the vertical frame with a blurred background.
03

AI Highlight & Peak Hype Detection

Click "Generate Clips". LumenCut transcribes the audio and ranks short speech windows using transcript density and emphasis cues. Scores are heuristic, not a virality guarantee.

Audio Volume Peaks Current highlight ranking uses speech activity, transcript length, emphasis words, and punctuation.
Review Before Posting Preview each transcript and rendered clip; edit the post title and caption before scheduling.
Virality Scoring The displayed 1–100 value is a relative heuristic to help compare the selected windows.
04

Video-Contextual AI Captions & Trending Tags

Rendered clips include the recognized transcript as burned-in captions. The user edits the post caption and title before publishing.

Transcript Speech recognition comes from Whisper; check names, Hindi/Hinglish spelling, and punctuation before publishing.
Editable Post Text LumenCut does not claim to find live trends or generate hashtags.
Platform Format YouTube and Instagram use the title and caption you review in the schedule dialog.
05

Connect Social Accounts (1-Time Setup)

Connect only after an administrator configures the Google and Meta developer apps and OAuth callbacks on the backend.

YouTube Data API Uses Google Cloud OAuth 2.0 to upload shorts directly to your verified channel.
Meta Graph API Uses the official Instagram Content Publishing API to schedule Reels automatically.
Account Requirements Google API quota, Meta permissions/review, hosting, storage, and compute availability depend on your accounts and provider.
06

Auto-Schedule & Automated Background Post

Choose a clip and platform, set a future date/time, review the final text, then confirm. The configured backend must stay available.

Peak Hours Picker Choose the date and time in your browser's local timezone.
Automatic Metadata Review and edit your title and caption; no trending tags are automatically added.
Automated Queue The queue publishes in the background only after your cloud service and platform OAuth are configured and working.

CORE TECHNOLOGY

Engineered for Serious Creators

The public site needs an administrator-configured backend before processing or publishing is available.

🤖

Speech Transcription & Clip Captions

Uses an open-source Whisper model to transcribe speech and FFmpeg to render those captions into a vertical video.

🚀

YouTube & Instagram Queue

Connect an account through configured OAuth, review post text, and schedule posts on the persistent backend queue.

🎙️

Speech-Based Highlight Ranking

Ranks short transcript windows with speech density and emphasis cues. Scores are heuristic and should be reviewed.

🎯

Vertical Video Layouts

Center crop or blurred-background layouts are rendered with FFmpeg. Automatic face tracking is not included.

💬

Burned-In Speech Captions

Whisper supports language detection and selected language hints; review Hindi and Hinglish recognition before publishing.

⚡

Hosted Backend Required

Compute, video storage, bandwidth, and platform quotas may have costs. Keep OAuth secrets in the host's secret manager.
