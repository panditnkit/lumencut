How to Use LumenCut

A step-by-step master walkthrough of every single tool, setting, and automated workflow.

01

Input Video / Stream Source

Feed any long-form video into LumenCut. The engine uses smart audio-first ingestion so you never have to download massive 40GB files.

URL Ingestion Paste YouTube videos, live streams, Twitch VODs, or Kick stream links.
Local Upload Drag & drop local raw recordings (.mp4, .mkv, .mov) directly from your drive.
Audio-First Extracts audio in under 10 seconds to detect highlights before touching the video.
02

Configure Engine Parameters

Customize how your vertical clip looks and sounds using the four precision control boxes in the engine bar.

Caption Style Choose between Emerald Mint (TikTok), Hormozi Gold, Cyber Lime, or Clean Minimal.
Speech Language Native support for Hinglish (Hindi written in English), pure Hindi, English, and 52+ languages.
Layout Mode Split: Puts facecam top & gameplay bottom. Dynamic Crop: Tracks moving speakers.
03

AI Highlight & Peak Hype Detection

Click "Generate Clips". LumenCut runs a multi-signal AI algorithm to pick viral moments without human intervention.

Audio Volume Peaks Detects sudden decibel jumps, screaming, laughter, and high-energy excitement.
Chat Velocity Scans Twitch and YouTube chat rate spikes (e.g. 500 messages/min indicates a viral moment).
Virality Scoring Assigns a score from 1-100 based on hook strength, pacing, and emotional payoff.
04

Video-Contextual AI Captions & Trending Tags

The AI reads the spoken transcript of each clip, writes high-converting social captions, and adds niche-specific trending hashtags automatically.

Contextual Caption Understands what happened in the clip (e.g. 1v4 clutch vs tech insight) and writes an engaging hook & caption.
Live Trending Hashtags Injects trending tags matched to the exact video topic (#gaming, #esports, #podcast, #reelsindia).
Platform Format Formats specifically for Instagram Reels (hashtags at bottom) and YouTube Shorts (clicky title + #Shorts).
05

Connect Social Accounts (1-Time Setup)

Connect your YouTube Channel and Instagram Creator profile directly to LumenCut with zero monthly third-party subscriptions.

YouTube Data API Uses Google Cloud OAuth 2.0 to upload shorts directly to your verified channel.
Meta Graph API Uses the official Instagram Content Publishing API to schedule Reels automatically.
Zero Cost No $30/mo Buffer or Hootsuite fee. Uses 100% free official developer endpoints.
06

Auto-Schedule & Automated Background Post

Click "Auto-Schedule" on any generated clip to open the scheduler modal and launch the automated publishing pipeline.

Peak Hours Picker Select date & peak audience engagement times (e.g. 7:00 PM IST for gaming).
Automatic Metadata Title, description, caption, and trending hashtags are auto-filled and sent to the platform.
Automated Queue The clip enters your queue and publishes automatically in the background while you sleep.
